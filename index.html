Ali Okan Yüksel — Enterprise Security Engineer at Tenable, holding OSCP and CEH. I work where exposure management meets threat hunting: turning Nessus and Tenable.sc scan data into a fleet-wide detection sensor, automating vulnerability operations, and hunting adversarial behavior at scale.
